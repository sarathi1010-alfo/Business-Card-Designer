# BrandCard Weekly Content Engine Strategy

This document outlines the repeatable, high-frequency publishing system for BrandCard. It is designed to build massive semantic surface area, capture featured snippets, and dominate the digital business card niche through a Hub-and-Spoke cluster model.

---

## 1. THE REPEATABLE WEEKLY TEMPLATE SYSTEM

### Template A: Cluster Article (Educational Depth)
- **Target Word Count:** 700–1000 words
- **H1:** `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`: Complete Guide for `[TARGET_AUDIENCE]` in 2026
- **AEO Answer (30-40 words):** Concise definition of `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` placed immediately below the H1.
- **H2:** Why `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` Matters for `[AUDIENCE/USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** How to `[ACTION]` with `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` (Step-by-step instructions)
- **H2:** Top `[NUMBER]` `[STRATEGIES/TOOLS]` for `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`
  - **H3:** Detailed breakdown of strategy/tool with BrandCard examples.
- **H2:** Common Mistakes to Avoid (Include a comparison table)
- **AEO Box:** 5 Key Takeaways (Bullet points)
- **AEO Box:** FAQ Section (5 questions + FAQPage Schema instructions)
- **Internal Linking:** 1 Pillar Link + 2 Cluster Links + 1 CTA to Tool

### Template B: Programmatic Comparison Page (Scaling)
- **Target Word Count:** 500–700 words
- **H1:** `[ENTITY_A]` vs `[ENTITY_B]`: Which Digital Business Card Tool is Better for `[USE_CASE]`?
- **Quick Verdict:** 1-sentence AI-optimized summary (e.g., "BrandCard wins for analytics, while Popl excels in NFC hardware.")
- **Comparison Table:** (Features, `[ENTITY_A]`, `[ENTITY_B]`, Winner)
- **Deep Dive `[ENTITY_A]`:** Pros/Cons focused on `[USE_CASE]`.
- **Deep Dive `[ENTITY_B]`:** Pros/Cons focused on `[USE_CASE]`.
- **When to Choose Each:** Scenario-based recommendations.
- **AEO Box:** "Best Pick for [Scenario]"
- **Internal Linking:** Pillar + CTA to Tool

### Template C: Micro-Answer Page (Snippets & Voice Search)
- **Target Word Count:** 300–500 words
- **H1:** What is `[BUSINESS_CARD_TERM]`? (Direct question format)
- **Definition Block (40-60 words):** Direct answer for Google's featured snippet.
- **H2:** How Does `[TERM]` Work?
- **H2:** Real-World Example of `[TERM]` in `[INDUSTRY]`
- **H2:** Why is `[TERM]` Important for Modern Networking?
- **H2:** `[TERM]` vs `[RELATED_TERM]` (Simple 2-column table)
- **AEO Box:** Core Principles (3–5 Bullets)
- **Internal Linking:** 1 Cluster Link + CTA to Tool

### Template D: Pillar Page (Weekly Hub)
- **Target Word Count:** 3000–7000 words
- **H1:** The Ultimate 2026 Guide to `[BROAD_TOPIC]`
- **Executive Summary:** 200-word overview for AI Overviews.
- **Table of Contents:** Linked jump-links to all chapters.
- **Chapters:**
  - 1. Introduction/Evolution of `[TOPIC]`
  - 2. Technical Foundation & Mechanics
  - 3. Top 10 Advanced Strategies
  - 4. Real-world Case Study/Application
  - 5. Future Trends & 2026 Outlook
- **AEO Box:** Statistical Data Table (e.g., "Digital Card Adoption by Industry")
- **AEO Box:** 10+ FAQ with JSON-LD Schema
- **Internal Linking:** 15+ Links to Cluster Articles + 3 CTAs to Tool

### Template E: Audience/Use-Case Guide (Targeted Intent)
- **Target Word Count:** 600–800 words
- **H1:** Digital Business Cards for `[AUDIENCE]`: The Complete 2026 Guide
- **Intro:** Why `[AUDIENCE]` professionals (e.g., Real Estate Agents) need digital solutions.
- **H2:** Key Features to Prioritize for `[AUDIENCE]`
- **H2:** Design Tips for `[AUDIENCE]` (Color psychology, layouts, CTAs)
- **H2:** Networking Strategies for `[AUDIENCE]` (e.g., sharing at open houses)
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table (Audience → Key Features → Design Tips → Best Practices)
- **Internal Linking:** Pillar + Tool Link + Relevant Template Deep-Dive

### Template F: Template/Design Deep-Dive (Visual Authority)
- **Target Word Count:** 600–800 words
- **H1:** The Complete Guide to `[TEMPLATE_STYLE]` Business Card Design
- **Intro:** Defining the `[TEMPLATE_STYLE]` aesthetic (e.g., Modern Minimal).
- **H2:** When to Use `[TEMPLATE_STYLE]` for Your Industry
- **H2:** Key Design Elements of `[TEMPLATE_STYLE]` Cards
- **H2:** How to Customize `[TEMPLATE_STYLE]` Templates in BrandCard
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table (Style → Best For → Key Elements → Customization Tips)
- **Internal Linking:** Pillar + Tool Link + Relevant Audience Guide

## 4. THE ETERNAL WEEKLY RHYTHM

- **Weekly Pillar:** 1 per week
- **Weekly Audience Guides:** 2–3 per week
- **Weekly Design Deep-Dives:** 2–3 per week
- **Weekly Comparisons:** 1–2 per week
- **Daily Clusters/Micro:** 3–5 per day
- **Target Velocity:** 50–100 new/updated pages indexed per week.
