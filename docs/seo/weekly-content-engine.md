# BrandCard Weekly Content Engine Strategy

This document outlines the repeatable weekly content plan for BrandCard to build massive semantic surface area in the digital business card niche.

## 1. THE REPEATABLE WEEKLY TEMPLATE SYSTEM

### Template A – Cluster Article (700-1000 words)
**Goal:** Daily educational depth and cluster support.
- **H1:** `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`: Complete Guide for `[TARGET_AUDIENCE]` in 2026
- **AI Overview Answer Block:** 30-word intro definition (for Google AI Overview).
- **H2:** Why `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` Matters for `[AUDIENCE/USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** How to `[ACTION]` with `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` (step-by-step)
- **H2:** Top `[NUMBER]` `[STRATEGIES/TOOLS]` for `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`
- **H3:** Detailed breakdown of each (with real business card examples).
- **H2:** Common Mistakes to Avoid (with table).
- **AEO Box:** 5 Key Takeaways (bullets).
- **AEO Box:** FAQ Section with 5 questions + FAQPage Schema.
- **Internal Links:** 1 Pillar + 2 Clusters + Tool.

### Template B – Programmatic Comparison Page (500-700 words)
**Goal:** Capture "vs" and "alternative" search intent.
- **H1:** `[ENTITY_A]` vs `[ENTITY_B]`: Which Digital Business Card Tool is Better for `[USE_CASE]`?
- **Quick Verdict:** 1-sentence AI summary.
- **Comparison Table:** (Features, `[ENTITY_A]`, `[ENTITY_B]`, Winner).
- **Deep Dive `[ENTITY_A]`:** (Pros/Cons).
- **Deep Dive `[ENTITY_B]`:** (Pros/Cons).
- **When to choose each:** Scenario-based recommendations.
- **AEO Box:** "Best Pick for [Scenario]".
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Tool.

### Template C – Micro-Answer Page (300-500 words)
**Goal:** Target featured snippets and voice search.
- **H1:** What is `[BUSINESS_CARD_TERM]`? (direct question format)
- **Direct Answer:** 40-60 word definition.
- **H2:** How Does `[TERM]` Work?
- **H2:** Real-World Example of `[TERM]` in `[INDUSTRY]`
- **H2:** Why is `[TERM]` Important?
- **H2:** `[TERM]` vs `[RELATED_TERM]` (comparison table).
- **AEO Box:** Core Principles (bullets).
- **Internal Links:** 1 Cluster + Tool.

### Template D – Pillar Page (3000-7000 words)
**Goal:** Weekly hub for a major topic cluster.
- **H1:** The Ultimate 2026 Guide to `[BROAD_TOPIC]`
- **Executive Summary:** 200 words for AI Overviews.
- **Table of Contents:** With jump links.
- **Chapters:** Introduction/Evolution, Technical Foundation, Top 10 Strategies, Case Study, Future Trends.
- **AEO Box:** Statistical Data Table (e.g., adoption rates, ROI stats).
- **AEO Box:** 10+ FAQ with Schema.
- **Internal Links:** 15+ Cluster Articles + Tool.

### Template E – Audience/Use-Case Guide (600-800 words)
**Goal:** Commercial and educational intent for specific segments.
- **H1:** Digital Business Cards for `[AUDIENCE]`: The Complete 2026 Guide
- **Section:** Why `[AUDIENCE]` professionals need digital business cards.
- **Section:** Key features to prioritize for `[AUDIENCE]`.
- **Section:** Design tips for `[AUDIENCE]` (colors, layout, CTAs).
- **Section:** Networking strategies for `[AUDIENCE]`.
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table (Audience → Key Features → Design Tips → Best Practices).
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Tool + Template.

### Template F – Template/Design Deep-Dive (600-800 words)
**Goal:** Highlight product capability and design authority.
- **H1:** The Complete Guide to `[TEMPLATE_STYLE]` Business Card Design
- **Section:** What defines `[TEMPLATE_STYLE]` design.
- **Section:** When to use `[TEMPLATE_STYLE]` for your industry.
- **Section:** Key elements of `[TEMPLATE_STYLE]` business cards.
- **Section:** How to customize `[TEMPLATE_STYLE]` templates in BrandCard.
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table (Style → Best For → Key Elements → Customization Tips).
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Tool + Audience Guide.

## 2. THE REPEATABLE WEEKLY CALENDAR

| Day | Theme | Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monday** | Fundamentals & Strategy | 1 Pillar Page (Template D) + 3 Cluster Articles (Template A) |
| **Tuesday** | Comparisons | 2 Comparison Pages (Template B) + 5 Programmatic vs Pages |
| **Wednesday** | Audience/Use-Case | 3 Audience Guides (Template E) + 5 Micro-Answers (Template C) |
| **Thursday** | Template/Design | 3 Design Deep-Dives (Template F) + 5 Programmatic Category Pages |
| **Friday** | Technical Features | 3 Cluster Articles (Template A) + 5 Micro-Answers (Template C) |
| **Saturday** | Audit & Linking | Planning next week + Internal linking audit + Schema validation |
| **Sunday** | Refresh & Prune | Content refreshing + Pruning low-performing pages + AEO optimization |
